Direction B — “One next step” as built

You picked B, flavour B1. This page has been updated to match what actually shipped: the strings are the ones prStatus() emits, the actions are the ones wired, and §8 lists where the original mock turned out to be wrong. Same real tokens as mockups/pr-actions.html (the A/B/C survey).

Where it landed. B1 keeps the +n more disclosure, and the objection to it (“that hides facts”) is answered by what the disclosure opens: the full fact list, each row with its own remedy button. The bar stays quiet; nothing becomes unreachable. The secondary facts cost one extra tap — that is B1's honest price, and it is written into the scorecard in §7.

1 · Pick the flavour of B

Three readings of “the bar whispers”, at the busiest state (red CI + 3 threads + 1 unpushed commit) and at the calm one.

2 · The dot does the work B's one graphic

B strips the chrome, so the single remaining graphic has to earn its place. Hue carries the verdict; an arc carries check progress — you get a sense of “how far” without C's gauge and without A's segments.

3 · Full state ladder as built · B1

Every state, as a sentence. Read the left column top to bottom — it should sound like a person telling you where the branch stands.

4 · The detail layer — where the pills went

In A, the pills became tappable segments in the bar. In B1 they move one click down: the sentence (or +n more) opens the full fact list, each row with its own remedy, then the raw CI checks. As built this is one widget — PrDetailBody — shown as a dialog on desktop and a bottom sheet on mobile, which is also what let the old pr_sheet.dart be deleted rather than kept in parallel.

5 · Placement

B is quiet enough to live inside the composer without crowding it — which is the placement A wanted too, and B has a better claim to it.

6 · Mobile

The sentence shrinks to the worktree row; the sheet becomes a single-focus screen — state, one action, detail behind a disclosure.

Same data, same width, same five states.

8 · Where this mock was wrong as built

Nine things only showed up once it was running. The pictures above have been corrected; this is the record of what they used to claim, because a reader who only sees the fixed page cannot tell whether a difference was noticed or missed.

9 · The three confirms

Only the irreversible actions ask — wrap up, discard and squash & merge — and each states what it will actually do rather than “are you sure?”. The merge dialog spends a line on what it will not do, because that is the assumption most likely to be wrong.

10 · Every action, exactly what it runs as built

The full behavioural picture. Do now actions run a command on the server, in the repo directory; Ask the agent actions insert the prompt below into the composer and stop — nothing is ever sent for you. <n> is the PR number, <base> is the PR's own baseRefName.
