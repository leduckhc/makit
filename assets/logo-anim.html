pino — mark animation

Exploring motion for the B4 mark. Each card loops the animation; tap Replay to restart. CSS-driven, so it translates directly to Lottie / Rive / Flutter AnimatedWidget.

Looping animations

Continuous motion — idle / ambient states.
A · Clap
Arms swing inward and back — the "clapping" metaphor made literal.
B · Breathe
Whole mark gently scales with a green glow. Calm, ambient.
C · Draw in
Head pops, arms draw in with stagger. Good for splash / first paint.
D · Wave
Arms sway back and forth, slightly staggered — a friendly wave.
E · Slide in
Arms slide in from the right with stagger. Snappy entrance.
F · Orbit
Whole mark rotates around the head center. Hypnotic, maybe too much.
G · Pulse ring
Rings expand outward from the head — "active / listening" state.
H · Acknowledge
Head squashes + arms snap together once per cycle — a playful "got it".
